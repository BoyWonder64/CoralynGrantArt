Final Reflection

The development process for this project was interesting. This is something that I have wanted to help do for my Mother-in-law for a long time. The main challenge I faced was getting the images to appear in a proper size. Most of these challenges appeared when I was working on the home page. What made this part difficult was she created layouts for me that she needed to follow to finish her final. A lot of the requirements in my personal opinion aren't great design-wise and look awkward. I got around this by doing a lot of image-sizing research and playing with a lot of different options. Because I was using the display:flex I resorted to using lots of containers and played a lot with width and max-width. I also broke down everything into columns, rows, and items. This made it a lot easier to know what was being updated and what wasn't.  

Once she finishes her course I want to redo most of her pages honestly. The main issue I have is how they needed to have their homepage laid out. I feel that fewer images and a bit more text should help make things make more sense. I think I would maybe take that chance to explain more about their work and what they do. Maybe include one or two examples. 
   
Overall I am happy with this project. It was pretty cool to not just deploy it on Netlify but also on a real domain that others can visit and see. Though it's not perfect, it looks pretty dang good.
